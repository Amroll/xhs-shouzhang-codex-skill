# Style Reference

Use this file as the visual anchor for all outputs. The goal is stable brand recognition across topics.

## Brand Positioning

Create a Xiaohongshu visual identity for Chinese literature, history, poetry, and reflective knowledge:

- guofeng scrapbook collage
- healing knowledge visualization
- collectible note-like covers
- warm literary atmosphere
- high information value without visual pressure

The visual should feel like a knowledgeable, gentle creator made a hand-crafted study page, not like a traditional poster, textbook page, fantasy game illustration, or commercial ad.

## Core Materials

Always prefer:

- warm off-white old paper
- vintage book page texture
- torn paper edges
- kraft paper fragments
- rice paper texture
- washi tape
- translucent tape
- sticky notes
- label stickers
- red seal stamps
- gold seal stamps
- handwritten arrows
- red and blue pen circles
- highlighter marks
- soft watercolor illustrations
- colored pencil edges
- faint ink wash landscape or plant details

## Color System

Primary base:

- warm off-white
- light khaki
- soft kraft brown

Accent colors:

- dusty rose red
- bamboo green
- muted teal
- ink black
- soft gold
- warm brown

Use accents sparingly. The page should feel calm, layered, literary, and collectible.

## Texture And Light

Use:

- soft natural light
- gentle paper shadows
- visible paper fibers
- layered collage depth
- slightly imperfect hand-made edges
- subtle ink and watercolor bleed

Avoid:

- glossy plastic texture
- hard dramatic lighting
- cold metallic shine
- flat digital poster look
- over-clean UI layout

## Typography Direction

Use visual text as part of the composition:

- large black brush-calligraphy title
- small red seal near the title
- handwritten note labels
- underlines and circles
- short sticky-note captions

Text should look hand-made and editorial, not like app UI. Keep major titles readable and visually dominant.

## Mood Keywords

Use these when describing atmosphere:

- 治愈
- 温柔
- 轻复古
- 国风高级感
- 文艺
- 知识感
- 收藏感
- 少女感
- 安静
- 有后劲
- 像认真做过功课的手账

## Consistency Rule

Every output should include at least five recurring brand anchors:

1. old paper or book page texture
2. torn paper or layered collage
3. tape or sticky note
4. handwritten annotation
5. soft watercolor or colored pencil guofeng illustration
6. restrained warm color palette
7. red seal or ink brush title
